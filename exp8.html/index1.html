Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball team sport played between two teams of eleven players on a large field featuring a central 22-yard pitch. Originating in 16th-century south-east England, the game requires one team to bat and score runs while the opposing team bowls and fields to limit the score and dismiss the batters. Governed internationally by the International Cricket Council (ICC), the sport is played in multiple formats, including traditional multi-day Test matches, 50-over One Day Internationals (ODIs), and fast-paced 20-over (T20) matches. It holds immense cultural and emotional significance in countries like India, England, and Australia, promoting teamwork, physical fitness, and sportsmanship.
